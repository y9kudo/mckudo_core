OvermindMC — Decision-making and automation library Minecraft Java. OMConnect — Independent client, included in the kernel and released separately. Author **y9kudo**, MIT.

### Major developments

- Simple API `createMinecraftAgent`: `get`, `craft`, `goTo`, JSON-Mission, Rescission and retention of experience.
- Local navigation and planning neural networks: Learning on results, Repetitive experiences, Maintaining World Weights and Agent. Mandatory limitations of geometry and prescriptions retained.
- Spatial memory, asynchronous A*, Total limited CPU worker for research and event response to threats.
- Diagonal, Walking around players, safe descent, Bridges with sittings, Construction under the feet and reversible descent on the pole.
- Protected areas and maintenance of building blocks; Combating, Impact recovery and shield orientation.
- Confirmed Lute Selection, craft from the server book, Training of stations, Smelting and planning for non-zeranite improvement/potions.
- Tasks and block editor/JSON on local web-Panels; group before 20 and coordination outside the game chat.
- The end of the work closes clients and the panel port even when memory is compromised. The verification of the composition of the packages and the installation of the archives in a separate annex are included in the CI.
